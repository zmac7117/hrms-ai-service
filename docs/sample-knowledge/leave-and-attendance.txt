How to apply for leave

1. Log in to the HRMS and open the Leave menu on the left side.
2. Click Apply Leave.
3. Choose the leave type (Casual, Sick or Earned), the from date and the to date.
4. Write a short reason and click Submit.
Your reporting manager receives the request. You get a notification when it is approved or rejected.

How to cancel a leave request

Open Leave, then My Requests. Find the request and click Cancel. Only requests that are still pending or approved for a future date can be cancelled.

Sick leave policy

Sick leave of more than 2 days in a row needs a medical certificate. Upload it in the same Apply Leave form under Attachments.

How to correct attendance

If you forgot to punch in or out, open Attendance, then Regularization. Select the date, enter the correct time and the reason, and submit. Your manager approves it.
